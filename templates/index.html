06 / Project notes

About GridSense

GridSense brings the project’s historical Formula 1 data and two-model prediction workflow into one focused race-analysis workspace.

Regression estimates finishing position. Classification estimates the probability of finishing in the top three. Both models are trained from the project dataset when the Flask application starts.
